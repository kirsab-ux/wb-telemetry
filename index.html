WB Telemetry

WB Telemetry is a personal, single-owner application for collecting, archiving and reviewing home automation telemetry.

Purpose

The application uses rclone and Google Drive authorization to transfer telemetry archives to the owner’s private Google Drive for storage and subsequent owner-authorized analysis. It is not a public service and this informational website does not offer account registration or access to telemetry.
