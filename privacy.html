Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026.

Data and purpose

WB Telemetry is operated for personal use by its owner. The application processes home automation telemetry, archive files and related integrity metadata. Google Drive authorization enables rclone to store, retrieve and manage the owner’s designated archive files. Depending on the authorized scope, Google may grant broader Drive permissions; the intended use is management of the designated archive area.

Use, sharing and storage

Telemetry archives are intended to remain in private storage controlled by the owner. The owner may separately authorize analysis through connected services, including ChatGPT. The application is not intended to sell data or use it for advertising. The public website contains no telemetry, credentials or access tokens.

Google user data is used to provide the requested file storage and retrieval functions. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Archive retention and deletion are controlled by the owner. OAuth authorization can be revoked through the Google Account third-party connections page. The owner can remove stored files and local credentials.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact shown in the Google OAuth consent screen. Do not post credentials or private telemetry publicly.
